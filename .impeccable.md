## Design Context

### Users
- Primary audiences: research collaborators and recruiters evaluating technical depth, reliability, and leadership potential.
- User context: quick portfolio scan on desktop/mobile to assess fit, credibility, and ways to contact.
- Job to be done: understand Lawrence's focus areas, verify quality of work, and initiate collaboration or hiring outreach.

### Brand Personality
- Cloud Architect
- AI Researcher
- Leader
- Emotional goals: intellectual rigor and calm confidence.

### Aesthetic Direction
- Tone: minimal, technical, and deliberate; content-first with restrained visual decoration.
- Theme: support both light and dark modes.
- Current visual language to preserve: monospaced technical typography, subtle motion, and clean spacing rhythm.
- References: not yet specified.
- Anti-references: not yet specified.

### Design Principles
1. Prioritize clarity of evidence: highlight projects, outcomes, and research credibility before visual flair.
2. Keep motion purposeful and optional: all animation must support comprehension and respect reduced-motion preferences.
3. Maintain calm hierarchy: use restrained contrast, spacing, and typography to reduce cognitive load.
4. Design for trust and contact: collaboration and recruiter pathways should remain obvious and low-friction.
5. Theme parity: light and dark experiences must preserve readability, contrast, and hierarchy equally.
